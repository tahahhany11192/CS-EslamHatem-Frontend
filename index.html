About The Instructor

About Islam Hatem

Islam Hatem is a Computer Engineering student at the German University in Cairo, currently in his fourth year. He earned his IGCSE certificate from El Rowad International School between 2019 and 2022, graduating with eight O-Levels graded between 8 and 9, and one AS-Level with grade A.

With five years of teaching experience, Islam has supported many IG Computer Science students through live sessions, private tutoring, assignments, and exam-focused practice.
